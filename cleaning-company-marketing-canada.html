Do you work with cleaning companies in any Canadian city?

Yes, remotely. Our work is done by video call and email, so location does not limit what we can build. We do not have offices outside of our remote setup.

Do I need to meet you in person?

No. Discovery calls, reviews and updates all happen by video call and email. Most of the work is a website and online accounts, which can be done from anywhere.

Do you handle French-language sites?

Not yet. Our work is currently in English only. If you need a bilingual site, tell us on the discovery call and we will say honestly what we can and cannot do.

Are prices in Canadian dollars?

Our package prices are listed with a $ sign. Please confirm currency and tax for your invoice on the discovery call so there are no surprises.

Where do you start?

We started in Alberta, with Calgary first, and now have city guides for 23 places across the country. If yours is not listed, book a call and we will talk it through.
