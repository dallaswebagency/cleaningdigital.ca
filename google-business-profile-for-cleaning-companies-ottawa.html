Google Business Profile for cleaning companies in Ottawa

In Ottawa, many searches end in the map pack, especially on a phone. Someone in Nepean types a cleaner near me and sees three listings, each with stars, a photo and a button to call. Your profile is your shop window there. If it has the right categories, a clear description, accurate hours and recent photos, it looks like a safer choice than a half finished one. We also look at how the listing compares with the other cleaners customers see beside you, so we can focus on the details that genuinely help you stand out.

Service areas deserve careful thought. Many Ottawa cleaners work from home or a small unit and travel across the city, so the address is hidden and the service area carries the load. We help you pick areas you can reach on a normal day, such as Centretown, the Glebe, Kanata or Orleans, instead of listing the whole region. Too many areas weakens the listing and invites enquiries you cannot serve. We check this against a normal driving day, not a map line, so the areas you list match the jobs your crews can really take on without strain.

Photos and reviews carry most of the trust. Real shots of crews, vehicles and finished work beat stock images every time, and a steady trickle of reviews tells customers you are active. We set up a simple routine for asking after each job and replying to each review. Seasonal updates, such as spring floor care or summer moves, give the profile fresh content throughout the year. We write the review request message for you, keep it short and friendly, and show your team how to send it so it becomes an easy habit.

Getting the basics of your profile right

We start with the details that are easy to get wrong. The business name should match your real name, with no extra keywords. The phone number should be one that is answered, and the website link should lead to a page that matches. Categories need a careful pick, since the primary one carries the most weight. We list every fix we make, so you can see what changed and keep a record of the details Google holds for your business.

We also check for duplicates, old listings and wrong addresses left from previous moves. Cleaning up these problems can fix visibility more than any new feature. If the profile is suspended or flagged, we help with the appeal and the evidence Google asks for, using your real business records. We review the entries again after a few weeks, in case customers keep asking about something the listing does not yet explain.

Services and descriptions that read like you

Add every service you really offer, each with a short plain description. Home cleaning, deep cleaning, move-out, office, post renovation or window work can all be listed separately. We write them the way you would explain them to a customer, without stuffing in city names or buzzwords. We also remove old or unclear photos, so the first images a customer sees are your best and most recent work.

The main description tells people who you are, what you clean and where you work in a few sentences. We keep it honest and specific. Mention the types of properties you handle and what makes your approach different, such as supplies provided or recurring plans. Skip claims you cannot support, like certifications you do not hold. We also suggest a quick routine your crew leads can follow, so asking for a review becomes a normal part of finishing a job.

Photos that look like your actual work

A profile with clear, current photos gets more attention than one with a logo and nothing else. We suggest a shot list: your team in uniform, your vehicle, supplies, and before and after pairs where customers agree. Photos of entrances, kitchens and offices help people picture the result. Each post needs only a photo and two or three sentences, so it is easy to keep going between busy weeks.

Keep images natural and well lit, and avoid anything that shows a customer's private belongings or address. Add new photos regularly, not in one burst. We build a simple habit around it, such as a few photos after each job. Over time that gives the profile a fresh and trustworthy look. We make sure each link and number in the listing matches your site exactly, because mismatched details can quietly weaken trust.

Collecting and answering reviews the right way

Ask every happy customer, soon after the job, with a direct link that opens the review box. A text or short email works well. Never offer a reward, never write reviews yourself and never ask only for five star ratings. Mixed, honest reviews look more real than a perfect run. We also keep copies of what we change, so you can see what was done and restore any earlier detail if needed.

Reply to every review, short and human. Thank people, mention the type of job and keep tone calm if a review is critical. A good reply to a complaint can win more trust than the review loses. Read our guide on setting up a service area for more detail on how location signals fit in. This gives you a clear view of which parts of the profile are worth the effort and which can wait.

Posts, questions and keeping the profile fresh

Short posts about seasonal offers, availability or a recent job keep the profile active. Before the spring thaw, a note about entrance and carpet cleaning fits well. Before summer, a post about move-out slots does the same. Keep them plain and useful, not salesy. We would rather share a modest, honest result than a bold promise that nobody can keep.

The questions section deserves attention too. Fill it with real answers about supplies, insurance, booking and areas, so customers do not have to guess. Update holiday hours well ahead of time, and check that special hours display correctly. Small updates show the listing is looked after, which reassures anyone comparing you with a competitor. Together those pieces make the listing feel looked after, which is what a customer checking on a phone is hoping to see.

Connecting the profile to your site and ads

The profile works best when it points to a strong page. The link should go to the page that matches the job, not always the homepage. If your pages are weak, our website design service can build service pages that give the listing somewhere good to send visitors. You stay in control of every edit, and we explain each change before it goes live.

We also track what the profile brings, using call and direction counts alongside website visits. Those numbers are not exact, and we explain their limits in each report. They show whether the listing is helping, and they tell us where to improve. We never promise a map ranking, only steady, honest work on the things you control. The goal is steady progress you can see, month by month.

Should I hide my address on my Ottawa profile?

If you work from home or customers never visit you, yes, using the service area setup. Google allows this for service businesses. Make sure your address stays accurate in your private records, and choose service areas you can reach in a normal working day. We help you set it up correctly so Google accepts it.

How many service areas should I list?

Fewer than you might expect. Pick the neighbourhoods and nearby communities your crews cover regularly. A short, accurate list is more useful to customers and avoids enquiries from places you would turn down. You can add areas later as you build reviews there. We check each area against your usual route before adding it.

What if a customer leaves an unfair review?

Reply calmly and factually, and offer to talk offline. If it breaks Google's rules, such as being from someone who never used your service, you can report it. Do not argue publicly. Future customers judge you by how you respond. We can draft a polite reply for you to review before posting.

Can I ask customers for reviews by text?

Yes, and it often works well. Send a short, friendly message with a direct review link soon after the job. Do not offer discounts or gifts for reviews, and do not pressure people. A simple ask is enough. We also write a short sample message you can copy.

Why did my profile disappear or get suspended?

Common causes are an inaccurate name, a questionable address or edits that looked like spam. We review what changed, gather proof of your real business and help you file an appeal. Results vary, so keep copies of documents like your licence and invoices. Acting quickly after a flag usually makes the process smoother.

How often should I post to my profile?

Aim for something regular but manageable, such as a short post or new photos every few weeks. Match posts to real events like seasonal cleaning or open booking slots. Quality and honesty matter more than posting every day. We can build a simple monthly reminder for your team.

Can I hide my home address?

If customers do not visit you, yes. Google allows hiding the address and showing service areas instead. We set it up correctly.

What if my profile was suspended?

Suspensions usually come from rule violations such as a wrong address or a name with extra keywords. We review the cause, correct it and submit a reinstatement request.
