A service-area profile lets you list the neighbourhoods and nearby cities you cover, such as Langley or Delta, without claiming offices you do not have. Reviews from landlords, families and builders

Surrey's mix of owner-occupiers, landlords with suites and builders needing post-construction clean-ups means reviews from several kinds of customer are useful. Ask landlords and property managers after turnover jobs, families after recurring cleans and builders after final clean-ups, each with a short direct link. Reply to every review, naming the kind of service. If someone is unhappy, respond calmly and offer to fix it. Do not buy reviews or offer gifts for them. Real, varied reviews help the profile match different searches without any tricks.

Photos, posts and consistent details

New homes and townhouses photograph well, so include real images of finished kitchens, bathrooms and post-construction results, with permission. Post occasionally about turnover or spring cleaning, and keep your hours and phone number current. Make sure your service area list, business name and website link all match what is on your site and invoices. Mismatches are a frequent source of profile problems. Can I advertise turnover cleaning for suites on my profile?

Yes. List it as a service with a short description of what is included, and link to a matching page on your website. Landlords and property managers search for it, and a clear checklist reduces disputes.

Do I need separate profiles for Surrey and Langley?

Usually not. One service-area profile can list both if you work in both. Separate profiles are for separate staffed locations, and extra profiles can look like spam to Google.

How do I show post-construction work?

Use real photos from finished jobs, with permission from the owner or builder, and describe what the service covers. Link to a page on your site that explains your process and how to request a quote.

How do I ask builders for reviews?

Ask the project manager or owner after the final clean-up, with a short direct link. Be specific about the project type, and avoid naming sites or clients without their consent.

Do I need to update my profile when I add a service?

Yes. Add the service with a short description, and add or update the matching page on your website. Keeping both in sync helps customers and avoids confusion.

Can I hide my home address?

If customers do not visit you, yes. Google allows hiding the address and showing service areas instead. We set it up correctly.

What if my profile was suspended?

Suspensions usually come from rule violations such as a wrong address or a name with extra keywords. We review the cause, correct it and submit a reinstatement request.
