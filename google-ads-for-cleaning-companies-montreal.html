Google Ads for cleaning companies in Montreal

Montreal's housing includes many duplexes and triplexes, older apartment buildings and newer condo towers, and each creates different cleaning needs. Late June and early July bring a spike in move-out and move-in cleaning because of the July 1 moving tradition, so we plan campaigns and budgets weeks ahead. Searches vary by borough and nearby cities such as Laval and Longueuil, and we set location targeting to match where your crews actually work.

We keep English and French campaigns separate, because mixing them confuses both the data and the customer. If your main market is French-speaking, the French campaign should come first, with a landing page that is properly written in French, not machine translated. Commercial work gets its own campaign and a quote form. We track calls and forms, review search terms regularly and report plainly on whether the English audience is large enough to justify a campaign.

Start with the French campaign, then test English

In Montreal, the order matters. We recommend getting a French campaign live first, with a French landing page that has been written or checked by a fluent speaker, and only then testing an English campaign alongside it. That way you learn where the main demand is before splitting a small budget in two. We are open about what we can and cannot do here: we manage the account and the structure, and the French itself needs a qualified writer.

Reading the results when two languages share one city

Keeping the languages in separate campaigns makes the numbers much easier to read. You can see the cost per call in each language and decide where the money should go. Be cautious about drawing conclusions from small numbers, especially for the English campaign, where the audience is smaller. We report plainly if one language does not justify its budget. Can you manage Google Ads in French?

We can structure and manage the account, but our own copy is in English. For French ads and pages, we recommend a fluent writer or translator we can work alongside, because poor French hurts trust.

When should I start ads for the July 1 moving season?

Plan well before late June. Ads need time to learn, and landing pages should be ready weeks ahead. We set budgets around your own booking history.

Is an English-only campaign worth it in Montreal?

It can be, for English-speaking residents, students and newcomers, but the audience is smaller. We test it modestly and report honestly on whether it pays off.

What if my French is not strong enough to write my own ads?

Work with a fluent writer or translator. Poor French can hurt trust more than having no French at all. We can brief them, set up the campaign and review the results with them.

Should I split a small budget between English and French campaigns?

Usually not at the start. We would put most of it into the language where most of your customers search, then add the second campaign once the first is performing.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. The account is in your name, ad spend goes directly to Google and you can see everything in it.

How much should a cleaning company spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your market, competition and goals. We agree a realistic starting budget on the discovery call and adjust from the results.
