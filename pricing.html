Are these prices in Canadian dollars?

Yes. All prices displayed on this page are in Canadian dollars (CAD), before applicable taxes.

Is website design a one-time payment?

Yes. Our website design packages are one-time project fees. Hosting is included for the first year where specified. Ongoing maintenance, hosting renewals and additional development can be quoted separately.

Is Google Ads advertising spend included?

No. Our Google Ads management fees cover campaign management and optimization. Your advertising budget is paid directly to Google through your own advertising account.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

Your advertising budget depends on your services, location, competition and business goals. We'll discuss a suitable starting budget before launching a campaign.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is a long-term process. Some improvements can be seen within the first few months, but meaningful organic growth often takes 3–6 months or longer depending on competition, website condition and your target market. No agency can guarantee specific rankings.

Do you work with cleaning companies outside Calgary?

Absolutely. Calgary is one of our priority markets, but we work with cleaning businesses across Canada, including companies in Alberta, British Columbia, Ontario and other provinces.

Can I purchase SEO or Google Ads without building a new website?

Yes. We can review your existing website and recommend the most suitable marketing plan. Your current website does not need to be redesigned if it already meets your requirements.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

Our marketing plans are designed to offer flexibility. Any initial commitment and cancellation terms will be clearly outlined in your proposal before work begins.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Yes. We offer website maintenance and updates to help keep your website secure, functional and up to date.

Can you help my cleaning company expand into new cities?

Yes. We can develop a location-based SEO strategy targeting additional service areas, with dedicated landing pages where there is enough local relevance and useful content to support them.

Will you guarantee more leads or first-page rankings?

No. We don't promise guaranteed rankings or a fixed number of leads. Our focus is on implementing a strong marketing strategy, improving visibility and optimizing your campaigns based on actual performance.

How do I get started?

Simply book a free consultation or send us an enquiry. We'll discuss your business, review your current online presence and recommend a suitable plan.
