Marketing for window cleaning companies in Canada

Window cleaning runs on routes, seasons and repeat customers. We help you fill the calendar with the right jobs in the right neighbourhoods, and keep them coming back.

How window cleaning companies win work online

Window cleaning is a route business. A crew that does six jobs on one street in a day earns far more than one that drives across the city for each. That changes how marketing should work. The best leads are not simply the most leads, they are the ones close to jobs you already have, and the ones that turn into a monthly or seasonal schedule instead of a single visit.

Demand also comes in waves. Spring and fall bring a rush of homeowners, while storefronts, restaurants and offices want steady, recurring service all year. A window cleaner who markets only for the spring rush spends the rest of the year chasing work, so the aim is to use those busy weeks to build a base of customers who rebook without being asked.

Common problems

Where window cleaning companies lose enquiries.

Seasonal swings make income uneven

Many window cleaners earn most of their year in a few busy months. If your website, profile and ads do not change with the season, you either miss the spring wave or pay for leads you cannot serve. Without a plan for off-season work, such as storefronts, gutters and exterior cleaning, cash flow becomes the main worry.

Leads come from too many places to serve efficiently

A job across the city costs you travel time. If your marketing is broad, you collect enquiries that fit badly with your routes and either turn them down or take them at a loss. A clear service area, with the neighbourhoods you actually want, saves time and fuel.

Residential and commercial buyers are treated alike

A homeowner wants a fair price and a friendly crew. A restaurant owner or property manager wants reliability, insurance, a schedule and sometimes high-rise or lift access. When one page tries to serve both, neither feels addressed, and commercial buyers rarely choose a company that looks like a side job.

Quoting is slow and awkward

Window price depends on the number of panes, storeys, access and how dirty they are. If the only option is to call or wait for a visit, people go to the next company. Many cleaners lose jobs simply because it took too long to get a number.

Opportunities

Where the growth is.

Win the neighbourhoods you want

Service area pages and a carefully set up Google profile let you appear in the places where you already have customers, so new jobs fit your routes. We help you choose areas by where your crews actually work, not by population alone.

Quote by photo

A form that lets customers upload a photo of the house or storefront, or enter panes and storeys, speeds up pricing and filters out jobs that do not suit. Customers appreciate a quick, clear answer, and you spend less time on site visits that go nowhere.

Sell the exterior bundle

Gutter cleaning, pressure washing, screen cleaning, solar panels and eavestrough work can sit beside windows. Separate pages and a simple add-on step in the quote form raise the average job and give you work when windows are slow. Pressure washing marketing covers the exterior side in more depth.

Lock in recurring customers

Offer a seasonal or quarterly plan at booking, and send a reminder before the next season. Storefronts and restaurants are natural monthly customers. A recurring list is the most reliable way to even out the year, and it costs far less than winning a new customer each time.

Our approach

How we market window cleaning companies.

1. Map your routes and best customers

We start by looking at where your jobs cluster, which customers rebook and which types of work are most profitable, so marketing is aimed at the jobs that fit your crews and trucks.

2. Set up the profile and service areas

Across Canada

Window cleaning looks different across Canada. In cold climates, the season is shorter and concentrated in spring and fall, with winter work limited to commercial storefronts and interior jobs. Milder coastal areas in British Columbia can run a longer season, while rain and moss change what customers ask for. Dense downtown condo towers in Toronto or Vancouver create high-rise and strata work that suburban markets do not have.

We write our city pages with those differences in mind, not as one template. See our approach for Edmonton, Vancouver and Toronto, or the wider Canada overview. If you also do inside cleaning for offices, office cleaning marketing covers that buyer.

How do window cleaners get more customers?

The reliable basics are a complete Google Business Profile with real photos, a fast mobile website with a quick quote path, and reviews from happy customers. After that, seasonal ads and recurring plans help fill the calendar. Which matters most depends on your area and routes.

Should I target homes or businesses?

Often both, but with separate pages, since they buy differently. Homes respond to reviews, photos and price clarity. Storefronts and commercial buyers want reliability, insurance and a schedule. Many cleaners use homes to fill spring and fall and commercial work for steady income year round.

Is Google Ads worth it for window cleaning?

It can be for seasonal searches, especially in spring, if your quoting is quick and your service area is tight. Many companies start with their profile and site, then add ads for peak weeks. We set a budget with you and report on cost per booked job.

How can I quote windows online?

Use a short form that asks for property type, number of storeys and panes or a photo upload, then give a range or a callback time. Be clear about what affects price. This filters poor fits and keeps good leads from drifting away while they wait.

Can I advertise gutter and exterior cleaning too?

Yes, if you offer them. Separate pages and a simple add-on option in your quote form help customers find them and raise the value of each visit. Make sure the crew, equipment and insurance really cover the work you advertise.

How much does a window cleaning website cost?

The cost depends on the number of pages, whether you need online quoting and how much content you supply. We offer fixed-price packages, shown on our pricing page in Canadian dollars, and confirm scope on a discovery call first.
