Local guide

Marketing a cleaning company in Surrey.

A fast-growing city with a lot of new homes

Surrey keeps building. New townhome and detached developments in Cloverdale, South Surrey and Fleetwood, plus tower projects around City Centre, mean a steady flow of first-time move-ins and builder cleans. Buyers collecting keys often want the place spotless before the furniture arrives.

A dedicated move-in and new-build page, written for homeowners and for builders, gives you something generic competitors do not. Our guide to post-construction cleaning marketing shows how to speak to those two groups differently.

Townhouses, basement suites and larger households

Many Surrey homes are townhouses or larger family houses, and basement suites are common. That creates real jobs: recurring cleans for busy households, deep cleans before and after tenants and turnover between renters. Larger households also mean larger scopes, so explaining how you price by size matters.

Our residential cleaning marketing guide covers how those searches work and what customers want to see before they call.

Surrey, Langley, Delta and beyond

Many cleaners in this market also work in Langley, Delta, White Rock and out toward Abbotsford. Each is a separate search, and a single Surrey page cannot match them all. We build pages only where you genuinely serve, with something real to say about each, and leave the rest off.

Because Vancouver is close, customers sometimes land on the wrong city's results. See our Vancouver page if you also work across the river, and keep your areas clear on every page.

Small offices, clinics and industrial space

Surrey Central, Newton and Guildford have many small offices, shops, dental and medical clinics and gyms that need steady janitorial work. Industrial areas across Surrey and Delta add warehouses and light industrial sites that need floor care and routine cleaning.

These buyers look for insurance, references and a written scope. A separate commercial page and a short request-a-site-visit form suit them far better than a homeowner booking flow. See commercial janitorial marketing.

Rain, moss and the wet season

The Lower Mainland's long wet season shapes the work. Mud and moisture come indoors, carpets and entryways take a beating and exteriors collect moss and grime. Spring brings pressure washing and window requests as people notice what the winter did.

We plan pages and ads around those cycles. Our pressure washing marketing guide is a useful starting point if you offer exterior work, and it pairs well with window cleaning in the same season.

Many languages, one clear message

Surrey is a multilingual city, and many customers feel more comfortable with a team that speaks their language. Our copywriting is in English only for now, and we will be honest about that. If your crew speaks Punjabi, Hindi, Mandarin or another language, your site can say so clearly, and it can be a genuine reason to choose you.

State only what is true. A simple line on the contact page about languages your team speaks is far better than a promise you cannot keep.

Reviews, trust and presenting price without a number

In a crowded market, trust decides who gets the call. Customers look for recent Google reviews, photos of a real team, proof of insurance and a clear idea of what the price covers. We help you ask happy customers for reviews on a steady schedule and answer every one. We never write fake reviews or invent testimonials.

On pricing, we do not publish your numbers without your say. Many Surrey companies explain how they quote instead, covering home size, number of bathrooms, condition and add-ons. It weeds out poor-fit enquiries and builds confidence.

Ads and booking for a busy, competitive market

Ads can bring enquiries quickly while SEO builds. Our Google Ads for Surrey cleaning companies focus on the jobs you want most, with the account in your name and a monthly look at cost per enquiry. If your site is not ready for paid traffic, we fix that first.

A short booking form that works well on a phone catches the customer who is comparing three companies on the bus or after dinner, before they settle on one of your competitors.

Is Surrey a different search market from Vancouver?

Yes. People usually search by their own city, and Google shows results based on where they are. A clear Surrey page, a correct service area and a complete Google profile help you appear for Surrey searches instead of being lost behind Vancouver companies.

Do I need separate pages for Langley, Delta and Abbotsford?

Only if you really serve them and can say something useful about each. We build pages where there is real demand and detail, and list the rest as service areas. Thin pages with swapped place names do not help.

Can you build a website in more than one language?

Not yet. Our work is in English only, and we will tell you plainly what we can and cannot do. Your site can still say which languages your team speaks, as long as it is true.

How do I get more first-time move-in and builder cleaning work?

We build a page for new homeowners and one for builders and developers, each with its own scope and quote path. Then we support them with search and ads for the neighbourhoods you serve.

Should I start with Google Ads or SEO?

Often both, in sequence. Ads can bring calls sooner, while SEO builds over months. If your website or profile is not ready, we fix those first so your ad budget is not wasted.

We have a website already. Do we need a new one?

Not always. We review what you have first. If it is sound, we improve pages and structure. If a rebuild is better, we keep clean URLs and redirect the old ones to protect what you have built.

Who owns the Google Business Profile and ad account?

You do. Ad spend goes straight to Google, the profile is in your name and we are added as managers. If we ever part ways, everything stays with you.

How do we get started?

Book a short call or send an email. We will ask about your services, areas and goals, then send a clear plan and quote. You can see the overall approach on our Canada overview.
