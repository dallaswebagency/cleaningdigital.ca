Google Ads for cleaning companies in Edmonton

Ads work best in Edmonton when they are narrow. We split campaigns by service, such as regular home cleaning, move-out cleaning, carpet cleaning and office cleaning, because people searching for each have different needs. Every group gets its own ads and its own landing page. That way someone looking for a move-out clean sees a move-out page, not a general homepage, and you pay for fewer clicks that go nowhere. It is not glamorous, but it keeps spending tied to real demand.

A spread out city makes location settings matter. If your crews are based on the south side, paying for clicks from the far north sends enquiries you may not want. We set areas around where you actually work, check which locations produce real enquiries and adjust. If you also serve nearby towns, we test them separately so a quiet area cannot quietly use up your budget. We revisit those settings regularly, because what worked in one season may not in the next.

We pay close attention to what people actually typed. Cleaning searches attract job seekers, people looking for products and those wanting free tips. We add negative keywords from day one and review the search terms report often. Over time we remove wasteful terms and add the ones that bring calls. It is slow, careful work, and it is where much of the difference between a good account and a poor one lies. Owners often tell us this part saves more money than any other single change.

How we set up an account for an Edmonton cleaner

We begin with your goals: which jobs you want, which you do not and how many you can handle. Then we build campaigns around those services and areas, write ads that match what people search for and set a budget you are comfortable with. Nothing runs until tracking works, so every call and form is counted. We also agree how you will tell us which leads turned into jobs.

The ad account is created in your name, and you keep access throughout. We explain what each setting does, so you are never left guessing. If we ever stop working together, the account, its history and its learning stay with you. That is how we think it should work. We also keep notes on every change so you can follow the story.

Writing ads that sound like a real cleaner

Good ads are specific. Instead of general claims about quality, we mention the job, the area, how quickly you can reply and anything that sets you apart, such as bonded and insured crews or eco-friendly products, as long as it is true. Clear, honest wording attracts the right clicks and filters out the wrong ones. Short, direct wording usually wins over anything fancy.

We test several versions and keep the ones that bring real enquiries, not merely clicks. We also add call and location details so your ad takes up more space and gives people a direct way to reach you. We never write claims we cannot back up, because they cause problems with customers and with Google. That patience pays off as the account learns what works in this market.

Landing pages are half the job

An ad can only get the click. The page decides whether it becomes a call. We build landing pages that repeat the promise in the ad, show a tappable phone number, a short form and a few proof points such as reviews and insurance. There is no menu to wander off through and no clutter. A focused page keeps attention where it belongs.

If your current page is slow or confusing, or hard to read on a phone, we fix it before spending much on traffic, because paying for clicks to a weak page wastes money. Our web design service can build dedicated pages for each campaign, or we adapt what you have if it is already solid. It is often the cheapest improvement available.

Residential, rental and commercial campaigns

Homeowners, landlords and commercial buyers do not behave alike. Homeowners often call quickly and want a fast price. Landlords look for reliable turnovers. Commercial buyers compare carefully and may take weeks. We run separate campaigns with different ads, pages and budgets, so one does not skew the numbers for the others. Mixing them together hides what is working and what is not.

We also track each separately. A commercial lead might be worth far more to you than a single home clean, even if it costs more to win. When you know the value of each type of job, you can decide where to put more money. We help you work that out with your own figures, not guesses. Good decisions need your real numbers beside the platform ones.

Budgets, bidding and when to hold back

A small budget spread across too many things does little. We start with the services and areas most likely to pay off, give them enough budget to gather real data and expand only when we have evidence. We adjust spending as your capacity changes, so you are not paying for enquiries during a week when your calendar is already full. We would rather prove a few things well than scatter spend widely.

We keep an eye on bidding settings and let Google's automation help only when there is enough conversion data to guide it. Until then we manage more carefully by hand. If the numbers say ads are not worth it right now, we will tell you and suggest putting effort into your Google Business Profile or website first. That honest view protects your money in the long run.

Reporting what the money actually did

Each month we show how many calls and forms came from ads, what they cost and which campaigns and search terms drove them. We separate genuine enquiries from spam and wrong numbers where we can, and we ask you about the jobs that closed so the picture reflects reality, not just platform numbers. We keep the report short enough to read over a coffee.

We never promise a number of leads or a cost per lead, because results depend on your market, your pages and your response time. What we promise is clear reporting and steady improvement. For the bigger picture, see our Edmonton marketing page and our approach to working with owners. Each month we agree what to change next, so the account keeps moving in a sensible direction.

How much should an Edmonton cleaner spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your market, your capacity and the value of a job to you. We start with a modest budget focused on your best services, learn from real data and adjust. We would rather start small and grow than burn through money on guesswork. Your own numbers decide when to raise or lower it.

Why do I get calls from people who just want a quote shopping around?

That happens in every service business. We shape ads and pages to set expectations, and you can speed up replies to win more of them. Tracking helps us see which campaigns bring serious enquiries so we can lean toward those. A quick, friendly reply often turns shoppers into customers.

Can I run ads only during business hours?

Yes. We can schedule ads for when someone can answer, and use call settings so phone leads arrive when you are available. Some owners prefer to keep forms running overnight and call back in the morning. We set it up to suit you. We can review the schedule after a few weeks and adjust it.

What if I get clicks but no calls?

We check the landing page, the targeting and the search terms. Often the page is slow or unclear, or the ads attract the wrong searches. We fix one thing at a time and watch the effect, and we tell you honestly if the numbers suggest pausing. Often a small fix to the page changes the picture quickly.

Do I need a long contract for ads management?

We would rather you stay because the work is useful. Ask us about terms before you commit, and ask how we charge. The ad account stays yours regardless, so you are never locked in by access. You can ask us about terms before you commit to anything.

Should I advertise my Google Business Profile as well?

Your profile appears for free in local results, so we usually fix it first. Ads can then support it for key services and seasons. We look at what you already have before deciding where extra spend would help most. Often a few tidy fixes there bring more calls than extra ad spend.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. The account is in your name, ad spend goes directly to Google and you can see everything in it.

How much should a cleaning company spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your market, competition and goals. We agree a realistic starting budget on the discovery call and adjust from the results.
