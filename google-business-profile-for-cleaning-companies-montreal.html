Google Business Profile for cleaning companies in Montreal

Most customers meet your cleaning company as a small box on a map. They see your name, rating, a few photos and a phone number, and decide in seconds. In a city of islands, suburbs and boroughs, the details matter: a service area that matches where you really work, categories that fit what you clean and photos that look like Montreal buildings, not stock images from somewhere else. Small details like these are what make a listing feel like a real local business instead of a generic directory entry.

Profiles in Montreal often carry a second challenge, which is two languages in one listing. A short English description serves English speaking customers, and French customers will expect French wording. We write and tidy the English side, keep business details consistent and flag the parts that need a fluent French writer. We never paste machine translation into a profile, because customers notice, and trust drops quickly. A little care here protects your reputation, and it costs far less than fixing a bad impression afterwards.

Profile work also has to respect how people book here. Tenants searching near the end of June need to see move-out cleaning listed as a service, with hours that match real availability. Landlords and condo boards look for photos of common area work and reviews that mention reliability. Property managers look for a phone number that someone answers. We build the profile around those buyers, using only information that is true for your business. Every detail we add is checked with you first, so the profile never claims something your team cannot deliver.

Categories and services that fit plex and condo work

Your main category should describe your core work, and secondary categories should cover real extras only. We then add services one by one: move-out cleaning, recurring home cleaning, condo and common area cleaning, post-renovation cleaning and office cleaning. Each service gets a short description in plain words so customers can see what is included. Accuracy here helps customers and search results alike, so we treat it as basic housekeeping and check it again every quarter.

We avoid stuffing the list with services you do not offer. A tidy, accurate list helps you appear for the right searches and sets correct expectations. If you add a service later, we update the profile and the matching website page together so the two never disagree. This is a simple way to look more specific than competitors who list only a single generic service.

Service areas across the island and suburbs

Google lets you list areas you serve, and the list should reflect your actual routes. Many Montreal companies include the island, Laval and parts of the South Shore. A long list that includes every town within reach looks careless and can confuse map results, so we keep it short and truthful. A short, accurate list is easier for customers to trust and simpler for you to defend if anyone asks.

If you work from home and do not receive customers, we help you hide the address and use a service area setup instead. This follows Google's rules and protects your privacy. We also check that your listed phone number and hours match the website, so customers see one consistent business. Small consistency fixes like these quietly improve how your business appears across Google.

Photos that look like real Montreal work

Customers trust photos of real jobs more than any description. We help you plan a simple set: your team at work, before and after shots with permission, your vehicle, tools and a few interiors that look like the buildings in your area. Faces and addresses stay private unless the customer agrees. Photos from different seasons, such as winter entry floors or a bright spring window clean, add useful variety.

Quality matters more than quantity. A handful of clear, recent images beats dozens of dark phone snaps. We suggest a monthly habit of adding a few new photos, which also signals to customers that the business is active and worth contacting. Fresh, clear images show that real people are behind the business and that it is open for work today, which matters more than polish.

Asking for reviews and replying in two languages

Reviews carry heavy weight in a city where people hire cleaners on recommendation. We help you ask happy customers at the right moment, with a short message and a direct link. We never offer rewards for reviews or write fake ones, because that breaks Google's rules and can get a profile restricted. We keep the process short enough that your team will actually do it every week.

Replies should be polite, short and specific. Reply in the language the customer used if you can do so well. We can draft English replies, and for French ones we suggest a fluent speaker so your tone stays natural. Answering every review, including critical ones, shows future customers how you handle problems. Public replies are read by future customers, so even a short, calm answer to a complaint can win you work.

Posts and updates timed to the calendar

Profile posts are a free way to show you are active. Around the end of June, a post about move-out cleaning with a clear call to action makes sense. In late autumn, a note about salt and slush on entry floors may help. We plan a light schedule so posting never becomes a chore. Keep posts short and specific, and update or remove them when they are out of date.

We also update holiday hours, new services and seasonal offers as they change. An out of date profile quietly costs enquiries, because customers assume a business with old information may not answer. A small monthly check keeps everything accurate. That is quick to do, and it keeps your profile looking like a business that is open and paying attention to detail.

Connecting the profile to your site and ads

The profile should link to the right page, not just the homepage. A move-out listing sends people to the move-out page, and a commercial listing to a walkthrough form. We add tracking so you can see calls and website clicks coming from the profile. Our local SEO service treats the profile and pages as one system. Seeing which actions customers take helps us decide what to improve first, without any guesswork.

If you run ads, the profile also supports them with ratings, photos and call buttons. See our Montreal cleaning company marketing page for how profile, site and ads combine, and we adjust the plan to your size and goals. We keep that plan simple, and we review it with you as your business grows or your service area changes. That way you always know exactly where each call and website visit came from.

Should my profile description mention both languages?

It can, if your team really serves both. Keep the English clear and short, and have a fluent French speaker write the French line. A mixed, machine translated description reads badly to both groups. We write the English part and tell you where French needs a native writer.

How many service areas should I list in Montreal?

As many as you genuinely serve, and no more. For many companies that means the island plus one or two nearby cities. A long list of far away towns weakens the profile and sets false expectations. We review your route and travel time with you.

What photos work best for a cleaning profile?

Real, recent photos of your crew, vehicle, equipment and finished rooms, with customer permission. Before and after shots of kitchens, stairwells or bathrooms help. Avoid stock images and anything showing house numbers or faces without consent. A few new photos each month is enough. We help you pick a handful and keep adding fresh ones through the year.

Can I get reviews from tenants after a move-out?

Yes. Ask once the job is done and the customer is happy, with a short message and a direct link. Do not offer discounts or gifts in return, as that breaks Google's rules. Tenants often leave reviews quickly because the result is fresh in their mind.

What should I post on my profile in June?

A simple post about move-out and end of lease cleaning, with a booking or quote link and your real availability. Add a few photos of finished units. Keep it short and factual. After the rush, replace it with a post about recurring cleaning. Keep it factual, with your real availability and a quote link.

Who manages the profile if I change agencies?

You own it. We are added as a manager, so you can remove us at any time and keep the listing, reviews and photos. We never take the profile under our own account, and we hand over a simple summary if you ever move on.

Can I hide my home address?

If customers do not visit you, yes. Google allows hiding the address and showing service areas instead. We set it up correctly.

What if my profile was suspended?

Suspensions usually come from rule violations such as a wrong address or a name with extra keywords. We review the cause, correct it and submit a reinstatement request.
