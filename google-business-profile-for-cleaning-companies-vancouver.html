Google Business Profile optimization for cleaning companies in Vancouver

Vancouver customers often search from a phone while comparing a few local cleaners. Your Google Business Profile is what they see first in Maps and the local results, so it should show the services, areas and photos that match how people in the city actually book a clean.

Google Business Profile for cleaning companies in Vancouver

Metro Vancouver is made of many cities, and customers often search by the one they live in, such as Burnaby, Richmond or North Vancouver. A service-area profile lets you list the places you serve without claiming offices you do not have. Decide early whether to hide your home address, and keep your phone number and website identical to what is on your profile. In a market with many condos and strata buildings, adding services like move-out cleaning and common area cleaning helps strata managers and owners find the right match. Our Vancouver marketing page covers the wider approach.

The wet climate shapes what people search for, so window cleaning, carpet cleaning and deep cleaning in spring and fall are worth listing as services. Posts about seasonal offers show the profile is active, and photos of real before and after work build trust, with the customer's permission. Reviews should come from real jobs, and replying politely to every one, including critical ones, matters. Avoid any shortcut that offers rewards for reviews, because it breaks Google's rules and puts the profile at risk. See also our Surrey marketing page for a nearby market.

Photos and posts that fit the coast

Vancouver's grey, wet stretches make bright, clean interiors and clear windows genuinely appealing, so photos of finished work do a lot of persuading. Take them in daylight where you can, get permission when a home is recognizable and rotate them through the year. Late winter suits posts about spring deep cleaning, and early fall suits posts about window cleaning before the rain settles in. Keep text short and specific, such as the type of job and the area, rather than slogans. A profile with recent, honest photos looks alive without needing constant posting.

Words that keep a service-area profile safe

In a region with many neighbouring cities, it is tempting to list every municipality nearby. Stick to the places your crews really travel to on a normal week. Use plain service names such as move-out cleaning or strata common area cleaning, and keep your business name exactly as it is on your insurance and invoices. Extra keywords in the name, or a fake storefront address, are common reasons profiles get suspended. Our local SEO service checks these details alongside your website.

Do I need a separate profile for each Metro Vancouver city?

Usually not. A service-area business with one base normally has one profile and lists the cities it serves. Separate profiles are for separate, staffed locations that customers can visit, and extras can look like spam.

Can strata companies find my profile?

They can if your services and categories match what they search for. Add common area and building cleaning as services, describe them clearly, and make sure your website has a matching page with a quote path.

What if my profile gets suspended?

Check the reason Google gives, fix the issue such as a wrong address or a name with extra keywords, and submit a reinstatement request with supporting documents. Having your details match your website and signage makes this much easier.

How many cities should I list as service areas?

List the ones you genuinely serve on a regular basis. A tight, honest list tends to work better than a long one, and it keeps your website pages and profile telling the same story.

Can I show before and after photos?

Yes, with the customer's permission and without showing personal details. Real images from actual jobs build more trust than stock photos, and you can add a short caption naming the type of clean.

Can I hide my home address?

If customers do not visit you, yes. Google allows hiding the address and showing service areas instead. We set it up correctly.

What if my profile was suspended?

Suspensions usually come from rule violations such as a wrong address or a name with extra keywords. We review the cause, correct it and submit a reinstatement request.
