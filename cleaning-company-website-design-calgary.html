How much does a cleaning company website cost in Calgary?

Our fixed packages are Starter $799, Business $1,299 and Premium $1,899 (CAD), each a one-time project. The pricing page lists what each includes. Larger builds are quoted after a discovery call.

How long does it take to build a cleaning company website?

Most sites take four to six weeks from approved design to launch, depending on content and how quickly feedback comes back.

Will my website help me rank in Calgary?

A well-built site gives Google the structure it needs, but rankings also depend on competition, reviews and ongoing SEO. We never guarantee positions, and we explain what to expect.

Can you add online booking or a quote calculator?

Yes. Every package includes a booking or quote request form. Fuller systems with calendar sync, pricing logic or deposits are scoped separately.

Do I need a separate page for each service?

Usually yes. A page for house cleaning, move-out cleaning, office cleaning and so on matches what people search and converts better than one page for everything.

Can you rebuild my old website without losing my Google rankings?

Yes. We map your old URLs to the new ones, keep what already ranks and set up redirects so you do not start from zero.
