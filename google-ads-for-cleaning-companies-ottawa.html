Google Ads for cleaning companies in Ottawa

Paid search is about timing and intent. Someone typing a search for end of lease cleaning in Ottawa wants help soon, and your ad can be the first one they see. We set up campaigns for each job type, so a move-out ad leads to a move-out page and an office ad leads to a commercial page. Mixing them in one campaign wastes clicks on people who wanted something else. We also discuss how quickly you can respond, because a fast call back often decides whether a paid click turns into a booked job or a lost one.

Where you advertise matters as much as what you say. Ottawa spans downtown, the older inner neighbourhoods, Nepean, Kanata, Orleans and rural edges that would take a crew a long drive. We set location targeting around where your teams actually travel, and we exclude places you do not serve. That keeps the budget on realistic jobs and protects your schedule from enquiries you would turn down. Before launch, we check each area against a real drive time from your base, so the map reflects your normal working day and not the full city boundary.

The calendar shapes spending. Spring thaw brings carpet and floor cleaning searches, summer and the start of the school year bring moves, and winter keeps entrance and floor care in mind. We plan campaigns ahead of those periods and ease off afterward. Between peaks, a smaller always on campaign keeps steady enquiries coming, and we review the search terms regularly so the budget does not drift. We keep a short written plan for each season, so you always know why spend is going up or down and can say yes or no first.

Choosing which Ottawa jobs deserve ad budget

Not every service suits ads. We start by listing which jobs earn you the most for the least hassle, such as recurring home plans, deep cleans, move-outs or office contracts. Those get the first campaigns. Low margin work or jobs that need long travel often do not justify paid clicks. We write down the reasoning, so you can challenge it.

We also check capacity. If your crews are already full, a big ad push only creates enquiries you cannot serve. Starting small and growing when you are ready is safer. You tell us how many extra jobs you can handle, and we shape the budget around that, not around how much Google would like you to spend. We revisit this list every few months, since the jobs that suit you best can change as your team grows.

Writing ads that sound like a real Ottawa cleaner

Ad text has little room, so every line counts. We write headlines that say what you clean and where, mention a real difference such as bringing your own supplies or taking recurring clients, and finish with a clear next step. We avoid hype, discount claims we cannot honour and promises about results. Short, plain wording usually beats clever slogans, and we keep a few versions running so we can learn which one customers prefer.

Your own business details also help. If you handle building access, keys or flexible timing for tenants, that can make an ad stand out. We use extra ad features like call buttons and location details so people can reach you quickly. Ads are tested over time, and we keep what performs and drop what does not. We keep every ad honest, because customers who feel misled by a headline rarely become repeat clients or leave a good review.

Landing pages that turn clicks into quote requests

An ad is only the first step. The page it leads to should match the search, load fast and show a short form and phone number right away. Sending every click to your homepage is a common and costly mistake. We build or adjust a page for each campaign so visitors see what they asked for. Even a small difference in the first screen, like a visible phone number, can change how many visitors take the next step.

If your current site is not ready, we say so before spending a dollar. Fixing the page first usually saves more money than any bidding tweak. Our website design service can build those pages so they fit your brand and work well on a phone, where many clicks will arrive. We would rather delay launch by a week than send paid visitors to a page that loses them straight away.

Keeping out wasted clicks

Some clicks are never going to become jobs. People searching for cleaning jobs, cleaning supplies, free tips or cleaners in another city can eat budget. We add negative keywords, review the search terms report often and tighten matching. In a city with a lot of job seekers and students, this ongoing clean up matters. Over time this ongoing clean up lowers wasted spend and leaves more of the budget working on real, relevant searches.

We also watch for competitors and directory sites that show up in the same results. You do not need to chase every auction. We aim at searches where your page is a good answer, and we pause terms that bring clicks but no enquiries. You see what changed and why in plain language. We share these changes in plain notes, so you can see the reasoning and ask questions at any point.

Tracking calls and quotes honestly

Before ads go live we set up tracking for calls from the ad, calls from the site and form submissions. Our guide to tracking calls and quote requests explains why this matters. Without it, you cannot tell which clicks became real jobs. If a number looks strange, we explain it, and we say so openly when the data is too thin to draw a firm conclusion.

Reports show spend, enquiries and cost per enquiry, split by campaign. We do not promise a number of leads or a fixed cost, because results depend on your market, your page and your response time. What we promise is clear reporting, and we use it to adjust. If a campaign is not earning its keep, we pause or rework it. You can pause or change the budget whenever your schedule shifts, and we will help you do it safely.

You own the account and set the limits

The ad account is in your name, and the spend goes straight to Google. We are added as managers, so you can look at the account any time and take it with you if we part ways. You set the monthly cap, and we do not raise it without asking. You can ask for a full walkthrough of the account whenever you like.

We also help with the part that sits after the click. Answering calls quickly and replying to forms the same day makes a big difference to results. Ads work best alongside a solid profile and site, so we connect this work to the rest of your Ottawa marketing plan, and we can talk through the order that fits your budget.

How much should I spend to test Google Ads in Ottawa?

There is no fixed answer. It depends on the services you promote and how competitive the searches are. We suggest starting with a modest test budget you are comfortable with, reviewing real enquiries and then deciding whether to grow, hold or stop. We often begin with a smaller, defined test, then review real calls before adding more.

Can I turn ads on and off with the seasons?

Yes. Many Ottawa cleaners run a steady small campaign and add budget before spring cleaning or summer moves. We schedule changes in advance, since new campaigns need time to learn, and reduce spend once the busy stretch ends. Seasonal changes are planned in advance, so you rarely have to rush or guess.

Will ads bring me jobs I cannot reach?

Not if targeting is set properly. We limit the map to areas your crews serve and exclude the rest. You can also tell us to pause ads when your schedule is full, so you are never overloaded with enquiries. If you need a day off from new leads, we can pause quickly.

Do I need a separate ad for each service?

Usually yes. A move-out search and an office cleaning search come from different buyers with different needs. Separate ads and pages let each message be specific, which tends to make the spend work harder than one general ad. We also compare results over time, so you see whether each one is truly worth the budget.

What happens if the ads are not working?

We look at the search terms, the landing page and the call and form data to find the cause. Sometimes the fix is a better page or tighter targeting. If it still does not make sense after a fair test, we recommend stopping and say so. We look at call and review data together, so each piece supports the other.

Do ads help my Google Business Profile?

They are separate, but they support each other. Customers often see an ad, check your map listing and reviews, then call. A strong profile makes ads more convincing, which is why we look at both together. We also keep the reviews you collect in mind when planning ad wording, so promises on the ad always match what customers say about you.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. The account is in your name, ad spend goes directly to Google and you can see everything in it.

How much should a cleaning company spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your market, competition and goals. We agree a realistic starting budget on the discovery call and adjust from the results.
