Google Ads for cleaning companies in Ottawa

Ottawa has a steady base of public service households, students, embassies and newer suburbs such as Kanata, Barrhaven and Orleans. Moves tend to cluster around university terms and summer, so move-out and end-of-lease cleaning get their own campaign. Because Ottawa sits across the river from Gatineau, some customers search in French. We discuss whether French campaigns are worth testing, and if so we pair them with proper French landing pages rather than translated ads sending people to English pages.

Commercial buyers here often have specific requirements around insurance, screening and reporting, so we keep an office and facility campaign separate, with a page that states what you can provide honestly and a quote request form. Winter salt brings carpet and floor cleaning searches into focus each year, and we plan budgets around that. Location targeting follows where your crews are based, and we review search terms and call tracking regularly before changing spend.

Timing ads around the university and public service calendar

Ottawa's demand follows institutional rhythms. Student move-outs cluster at the end of terms, and many households move in summer. We plan move-out campaigns to start before those dates, since ads need time to learn, and we pull budget back once the rush is over. Commercial enquiries are steadier, so they get a quieter, always-on campaign with its own tracking rather than competing with the seasonal ones.

Language, wording and what to measure

Even when ads run only in English, the way people describe the work can differ, with some searchers using French terms for the same service. We check search terms for this and decide whether any French wording is worth adding to the campaign. For measurement, we separate residential calls and commercial form requests so you can see which audience the spend is really reaching. See also our Ottawa marketing page.

Should my Ottawa ads be in French as well as English?

Not always. Many customers search in English, but some do search in French. If you test French, the landing page should be in French too. We usually start with English and add French where there is clear demand.

Can I advertise to government or embassy buyers?

You can reach people who search, but those buyers often follow formal procurement. Ads can help you get found, while contracts follow their own process that you manage directly.

Does Gatineau need its own campaign?

Only if you serve it. It is mostly French-speaking, so it would need French ads and pages, and we would treat it as a separate test.

When should I start my move-out ads in Ottawa?

Begin a few weeks before the main moving periods so the campaign has time to learn. We use your own booking history to choose the dates and reduce spend once the rush passes.

How do I know if French search terms matter for my business?

We review the search terms report. If a meaningful number of clicks use French wording, it is worth testing a French campaign with a French page. If not, we stay in English and watch the data.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. The account is in your name, ad spend goes directly to Google and you can see everything in it.

How much should a cleaning company spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your market, competition and goals. We agree a realistic starting budget on the discovery call and adjust from the results.
