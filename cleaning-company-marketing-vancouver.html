Local guide

Marketing a cleaning company in Vancouver.

Towers, strata and the buildings that shape your work

Vancouver is a city of condo towers and strata corporations, from Coal Harbour and Yaletown to the West End and Mount Pleasant. That changes who hires you. Often it is not the resident but a strata council or a property manager, and they want to see insurance, references and a calm, organized way of working. We show what a good page includes before we write a word, so you can add real details from your own jobs.

A page built for strata and building managers sets you apart from cleaners whose site only talks to homeowners. It can cover common areas, move-in and move-out cleans, scheduling around building rules and how a quote request works. If you already clean in towers, collect a few details worth sharing: how you book elevators, how your team signs in, how keys and fobs are handled and what you do when a building changes its rules. Owners and managers notice this level of care, and it is exactly what a generic cleaning site leaves out.

Neighbourhoods, laneways and character homes

Kitsilano, Commercial Drive, Strathcona and much of East Vancouver have older houses, suites and laneway homes. Customers there often want a regular clean, a deep clean before a rental, or help after a renovation. They search by neighbourhood name, so one page that says Vancouver cannot catch all of it. Those details are what separate your page from every other cleaner who lists the same neighbourhoods. Good local SEO for Vancouver cleaning companies decides which neighbourhoods deserve a page of their own, and writes each around something true about that part of the city.

Suites and secondary homes add another layer. A landlord with a basement suite wants a turnover clean between tenants, while the family upstairs wants a regular visit. A short note on each service, with a clear way to ask for a quote, lets both find the right door on your site without calling to ask.

Burnaby, Richmond, the North Shore and beyond

Metro Vancouver is many cities, and people search for them separately. Burnaby, Richmond, North Vancouver, West Vancouver and Coquitlam are each their own search, with different housing and different expectations. If you travel there, we help you decide whether a page or a simple service area list makes more sense. Each of those cities is worth treating on its own terms. Surrey has its own market too, so see our Surrey page if you work south of the Fraser. For other BC markets, our Victoria page and Kelowna page show how we handle them.

A practical test is whether you can name a real job, a building type or a typical customer in that city. If you can, a page is worth building. If you cannot, a line in your service area list is more honest and avoids thin pages that Google tends to ignore anyway.

Rain, moisture and the wet-season calendar

Vancouver has a long, mild, wet season, and it shapes the questions customers ask. Muddy shoes and wet floors, damp window frames, moss on walkways and general worries about mould all show up in conversations. Be honest about what your team does and does not treat, and say so plainly on the page. That honesty also helps you avoid enquiries you cannot fulfil. When the rain eases, window, gutter and pressure washing enquiries pick up. Our window cleaning marketing guide and the seasonal marketing calendar help you plan pages and offers before the busy weeks, not during them.

Entrance matting, boot trays and drying times are small details customers care about in a wet climate. If your team has a process for them, it belongs on your site. It shows you understand how people actually live here and gives your photos and descriptions something specific to say.

Move-outs, rentals and short stays

Vancouver has a big rental market and plenty of people moving between suites, condos and townhouses. End-of-tenancy cleaning is often tied to getting a damage deposit back, so customers want a clear checklist and a fast quote. A dedicated move-out page with a short form fits that need. Where short-term rentals are allowed, hosts need quick turnovers with fresh linens and reliable timing. If you do that work, a page explaining how you handle it will attract the right owners. Our move-in and move-out marketing guide goes further. Hosts value a calm, predictable crew above a low price.

Timing is the hard part. Many tenancies end on the same days each month, so crews fill up fast and enquiries arrive at the last minute. Having a ready quote path, clear lead times and ads switched on before the month-end rush keeps your calendar full without scrambling.

Offices, restaurants, studios and building work

Downtown offices, restaurants, studios and production spaces create commercial demand that does not search like a homeowner. They want proof of insurance, consistent staffing, clear after-hours arrangements and a written scope. A separate commercial page with a short site-visit request form fits them far better than a home booking flow. A concise page can answer all of this in under a minute of reading. Construction is a constant part of the city, so builders and developers also need final cleans. See our guides to commercial janitorial marketing and post-construction cleaning marketing.

Restaurants and cafes have their own needs, such as kitchen floors, washrooms and early-morning access. Film and production spaces need flexible hours. If you serve those clients, give each a short explanation and a way to ask for a site visit, so the right buyer sees themselves on your page.

Reviews, trust and showing your pricing without numbers

Vancouver customers compare closely. They read recent reviews, look at photos of real crews and want to know the price will not change on the day. We help you ask happy customers for reviews on a steady schedule and reply to every one. We never write fake reviews or make up testimonials. Replying to every review, including the occasional critical one, shows future customers how you handle problems. You do not have to publish prices. Many cleaners explain how they quote instead, covering suite size, bathrooms, pets, condition and extras like ovens and fridges. That filters out poor-fit enquiries and builds trust before anyone calls.

Photos matter as much as words. A team in uniform, a van with your name and a real before and after tell a buyer more than a stock image. We help you gather them respectfully and only with the customer's permission, so your page feels like a real company, because it is one.

Ads and booking for a crowded market

Searches for cleaners in Vancouver are busy, and paid clicks can add up quickly if the account is loose. Our Google Ads for Vancouver cleaning companies focus on the jobs you want most, with the account in your name and a monthly look at cost per enquiry. If your website is not ready for paid traffic, we fix that first. A fast mobile page and a short quote form do more for cleaning company marketing in Vancouver than a bigger budget pointed at a weak page. The page should load quickly on a phone, because that is where most searches happen.

We also recommend keeping early campaigns narrow. Start with the one or two services that make you the most money, such as move-out cleaning or recurring home cleaning, watch the results for a few weeks and widen only when the numbers justify it.

How do I get strata and property management work?

Start with a page written for them, covering insurance, scheduling, common area scope and how to request a quote. Then make sure your Google profile and reviews back it up. Strata decisions often take time, so we do not promise wins, only that you will look credible when you are considered.

Do I need a separate page for Burnaby and Richmond?

Only if you really serve them and have something true to say about each. Otherwise a strong service area section on one page is better than several near-identical pages. We review where you work and where search interest exists before deciding.

How long does local SEO take for a Vancouver cleaner?

It depends on your starting point and how busy your neighbourhood is. Profile and page fixes can show movement within weeks. Steady rankings in a competitive market usually take months. We report what we see each month and will not guarantee a position.

Should I start with Google Ads or SEO?

Often both, in order. Ads bring enquiries quickly while SEO builds, but only if your site converts visitors. If your website or profile is not ready, we fix that first so budget is not wasted on clicks that go nowhere.

Can you help with Airbnb and short-term rental cleaning?

Yes, where that work is allowed and you actually do it. We can build a page explaining turnovers, linens, timing and how hosts book you. We will not invent claims, so we start with what your team really offers. We start with what you already offer, then build from there.

We already have a website. Do we need a new one?

Not always. We review it first. If the structure is sound, we improve pages and speed. If a rebuild is better, we keep URLs tidy and redirect old pages. See our Vancouver website design service for how we approach it.

Who owns the Google Ads account and Business Profile?

You do. Ad spend goes straight to Google from your account, and the profile stays in your name. We are added as managers and can be removed at any time, so nothing is held back if you decide to move on.
