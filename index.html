Do you only work with cleaning companies in Calgary?

No. Calgary is an important market for CleaningDigital, and we also work with cleaning businesses in other parts of Canada. Our services can be delivered remotely, depending on the project and business requirements.

What services do you offer to cleaning businesses?

We provide website design, branding and logo design, Local SEO, organic SEO, Google Business Profile support and Google Ads management.

Can you redesign my existing cleaning company website?

Yes. We can review your existing website and recommend improvements to its design, messaging, mobile experience, search foundations and enquiry process.

Do you offer website design as a one-time project?

Yes. Website design is available as a one-time project with a defined scope. Hosting, renewals, maintenance and additional development should be confirmed in the proposal.

Is Google Ads spend included in your management fee?

No. The Google Ads management fee covers campaign management. The advertising budget is paid separately by the client to Google.

How long does SEO take to show results?

SEO is an ongoing process. The timing depends on your website, competition, location, service mix and the work required. Some improvements may appear in the first few months, while stronger organic growth can take longer. Rankings and lead volumes cannot be guaranteed.

Can you help us target more than one city?

Yes. We can plan a location-based SEO strategy for the areas your business serves. Each location page should provide useful, locally relevant information rather than simply repeating the same content with a different city name.

How do we get started?

Book a consultation or send an enquiry. We'll learn about your business and goals, review your current online presence and recommend a suitable next step.
