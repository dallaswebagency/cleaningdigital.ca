Do you only work with cleaning companies?

Yes. Every website, brand and marketing plan we build is for cleaning businesses, from maid services and janitorial companies to carpet, window and pressure washing crews. We already know how your customers search and what makes them request a quote.

How much does a cleaning company website cost?

Our fixed website packages are a one-time price: Starter $799, Business $1,299 and Premium $1,899 (CAD). Extras like more pages or a multi-location site are quoted before any work starts. Local SEO and Google Ads management are separate monthly plans, and ad spend is paid directly to Google.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or a set number of leads?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. We build on solid foundations, including clear service pages, fast loading, schema, Google Business Profile and reviews, then measure calls and quote requests so you can see what is working. Results depend on your market and competition.

Where are you based, and can you work with a company outside Calgary?

We work remotely by video call and email, so your location is not a barrier. Our focus is Calgary and Alberta, and we also work with cleaning companies across Canada. Nakul Chandra is your main point of contact from the first call.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We review your current site and Google presence, then tell you honestly whether targeted fixes or a redesign makes more sense. You can ask for a free website review when you book a discovery call.

Where should a cleaning company start: website, SEO, Google Business Profile or Google Ads?

Most owners start with a website that turns visitors into quote requests, then add local SEO and a strong Google Business Profile. Google Ads can bring in work sooner once the website is ready to convert it. We tell you what to do first and what can wait.
