Do I need separate pages for Halifax and Dartmouth?

Only if you serve both and can say something useful about each. Many cleaners begin with one page and list nearby communities as service areas, then add more pages as business grows.

When should I publish move-out cleaning pages near the universities?

A few weeks before the end of the term, so the page has time to be found. Students decide quickly, so keep the booking step short and the checklist clear.

Is local SEO worth it in Atlantic Canada?

Often yes. Competition is lighter than in the largest cities, so a complete profile and clear pages can stand out. Results vary and take time, but the groundwork is worth doing.

How early should I publish a student move-out page in Halifax?

A few weeks before the end of the term is a good rule. That gives the page time to be found and gives you time to plan your crews.

Can I list Dartmouth and Bedford on my homepage?

Yes, if you serve them. A short coverage line is helpful, and you can add dedicated pages later when you have local detail to share.

How long does a cleaning company website take to build?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback arrive. We agree a schedule after the discovery call and tell you what we need at each stage.

Will I be able to edit my own website?

Yes, if you want to. We can set the site up so you can change text, photos and offers, or look after it through a support plan.
