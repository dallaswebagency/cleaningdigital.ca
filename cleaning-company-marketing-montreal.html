Local guide

Marketing a cleaning company in Montreal.

French is the main language of business

Montreal customers mostly search and buy in French, and French is the main language of business in Quebec. Our site is currently in English, so this page serves English-speaking owners and customers. If your main market is French-speaking, we recommend planning French pages, and we are happy to talk through the options on a call.

Plexes, older buildings and condos

Housing in Montreal includes many duplexes and triplexes, older apartment buildings and newer condo towers. Cleaning needs differ between them, from tenant move-outs in older buildings to common area cleaning in condos. Clear service pages that match each situation help customers choose.

July 1 and the moving season

Moving day on July 1 is a well known tradition in Quebec, which means late June and early July bring a surge of move-out and move-in cleaning. Planning and publishing pages for those services before the season gives search engines time to pick them up.

Searching by borough and nearby cities

Customers often search with a borough or nearby city name, such as Plateau, Verdun, Laval or Longueuil. We help decide which areas deserve their own pages. See also our Quebec City page.
