Google Business Profile for cleaning companies in Edmonton

When someone in Edmonton searches for a cleaner on their phone, the map results appear before most websites. Your profile is what shows there: your name, reviews, photos, hours and a call button. We make sure every part is complete and accurate, from the categories and service list to the description. A thin profile looks inactive, and customers skip it for one that feels more alive and more trustworthy. The first impression takes seconds, so every detail should be right.

Because many cleaners work at customers' homes and do not welcome walk-ins, we set the profile up as a service area business where that fits and keep your address private. We then add the parts of Edmonton and nearby towns you serve. This is where honesty pays off. Listing places you do not really cover brings enquiries you cannot take and can trigger problems with Google. It is a small decision that protects your privacy and your reputation. We also check that the phone number rings somewhere a person can answer, since missed calls from the map are easy to lose.

Edmonton customers also use the profile to check you out before calling. They scan recent reviews, look at photos of real work and see whether you reply. We set up a gentle routine for collecting reviews from satisfied customers, with a direct link they can tap, and we draft replies that sound like you. Over months, that steady activity is what makes a profile feel established. Customers can sense when a business is paying attention. A quick, human reply to a review or question often tips an undecided visitor toward calling you first.

Getting the basics right on day one

We start by claiming or verifying the profile, then check the business name, phone number, hours and website link against your site and other listings. Small differences are common and quietly hurt you. We fix them and choose a main category that reflects the work you most want, adding others only where they truly apply. It sounds basic, but it is where many profiles quietly go wrong.

Next, we write a description in plain words, list your services with short explanations and add attributes that fit, such as being women-owned or offering online estimates if either is true. We never stuff the name with keywords, because Google treats that as a violation and it can lead to suspension. A plain, honest profile outperforms an overworked one in the long run.

Photos that show real work

Customers look at photos to judge whether a company is credible, especially when they are choosing someone to enter their home or workplace. We help you build a library of real images: your crew in uniform, your vehicle, equipment and finished spaces, with permission where customers or homes are visible. We avoid stock images and anything that misrepresents who you are. Photos also give customers a quick feel for your style.

Photos should be added steadily, not all at once. A few new images each month signal an active business and give people more to look at. We suggest simple habits, such as a quick photo at the end of each job, so the library grows without taking much of your day. Over a year, that adds up to a library that tells your story better than any description could.

Posts and updates that are worth reading

Posts let you share offers, seasonal reminders and news. We keep them short and useful, such as a note about booking spring cleaning early or a reminder about move-out cleaning availability. Each post links to a relevant page on your site, which gives visitors a path to follow and connects your listing to your website. Short posts with a clear next step tend to be read more than long ones.

We do not push daily posting, because quality matters more than volume, and a feed that looks forced puts people off. A modest, regular schedule is easier for you to keep and looks natural to anyone checking. A modest, regular schedule is easier to keep and looks natural. Handling reviews, good and bad

Reviews are the strongest part of a profile. We make asking easy by giving you a direct link and wording you can send by text or email right after a job. Ask at a natural moment, when the customer is happy, and never offer rewards for reviews. Google can remove them and penalize the listing. Consistency matters more than any clever wording.

When a review is negative, a calm, specific reply helps more than a defensive one. Acknowledge the problem, offer a way to fix it and move the conversation off the page. Future customers read how you respond. We draft replies with you and keep the tone consistent with your brand. A good reply often wins back more trust than the review cost you.

Questions, messages and keeping the listing accurate

Customers can ask questions and send messages from your profile, and many Edmonton shoppers do before they ever pick up the phone. If nobody answers, it looks neglected. We help you decide whether to turn messaging on, set up a quick reply and add answers to common questions yourself, such as whether you bring supplies or how you handle keys. Clear answers upfront save you repeat calls.

We also watch for edits from others. Anyone can suggest changes to a listing, and wrong hours or a changed phone number can slip in. A regular check keeps things correct. If something goes wrong, such as a suspension, we know how to gather evidence and appeal. Our guide to service areas covers a common cause. A quick monthly look is usually enough to catch trouble early.

How we measure the profile honestly

Google shows how many people called, requested directions or clicked through to your site from the profile. We track these alongside your own call records, so we can see whether more people are getting in touch. We do not promise a spot in the top three, because local rankings shift with distance and many other factors. Numbers are most useful when we read them alongside the jobs you actually booked.

We compare like periods and note what changed in each one, such as new photos, more reviews or a corrected category. That helps us find what moves the needle for your business. The profile works best alongside your website and ads, so our Edmonton marketing page shows how they fit together. It keeps the focus on what you can control.

Do I need a physical office to have a Google Business Profile in Edmonton?

No. If you travel to customers, you can set up as a service area business and hide your address. Google still wants the details to be genuine, so we help you follow its rules. Do not use a fake address or a virtual office. If you do meet customers at a location, we set that up differently.

Why did my profile disappear or get suspended?

Common causes include keyword stuffed names, a fake or shared address, edits Google flagged or mismatched details. We review what changed, correct it and file an appeal with supporting evidence. Results vary, but a clean, honest listing is the best protection. We also keep a record of the original details so an appeal is quicker.

How often should I post on my profile?

A steady, modest rhythm works better than bursts. Many cleaners do well posting a couple of times a month, tied to seasons or offers. We suggest a schedule you can keep, and we can draft the posts for you. Consistency matters more than frequency, so pick a pace you can keep.

Can I respond to old reviews?

Yes, and it is worth doing. A thoughtful reply to an older review still shows future customers that you care. Keep replies short, thank people and address any concern without arguing. We can help you work through a backlog. It also shows reviewers that you read what they wrote.

Is it okay to ask customers to leave reviews on the spot?

Yes, asking is fine, as long as you do not offer rewards or only ask people you expect to be positive. A direct link sent right after the job works well. We help you word the request so it feels natural. Timing helps, so ask while the clean is still fresh in their mind.

Should I add every service I can think of?

No. List the services you actually want to be hired for and describe them clearly. A long, vague list can dilute your profile and attract jobs you do not want. We choose a focused set and expand it as your business grows. Quality of description matters more than quantity on the list.

Can I hide my home address?

If customers do not visit you, yes. Google allows hiding the address and showing service areas instead. We set it up correctly.

What if my profile was suspended?

Suspensions usually come from rule violations such as a wrong address or a name with extra keywords. We review the cause, correct it and submit a reinstatement request.
