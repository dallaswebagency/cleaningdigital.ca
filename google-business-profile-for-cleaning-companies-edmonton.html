Google Business Profile for cleaning companies in Edmonton

Many Edmonton cleaners cover the city plus St. Albert, Sherwood Park, Spruce Grove or Leduc. A service-area setup lets you list those places honestly and keep your home address private if customers never visit you. Choose categories that fit your main work, whether that is house cleaning, janitorial services or carpet cleaning, and add the rest as secondary categories or services. Institutional buyers such as offices, clinics and campuses look for proof you are insured and reliable, so add a clear description and link to a page on your website that explains your process. See our Edmonton marketing page.

Long winters bring salt and grit indoors, so floor and carpet cleaning matter in late winter, and spring brings window and deep cleaning. A few well timed posts and fresh photos tell customers you are active and ready for the season. Reviews should come from real customers, with a simple direct link and a polite reply to each. Be careful not to list towns you do not serve just to look bigger, because mismatched details are a common cause of listing problems. Our local SEO service and Red Deer page show how this extends beyond the city.

Answering reviews in a practical market

Edmonton customers tend to value straightforward, dependable service, and that tone suits your replies too. Thank people by name, mention the specific job, and keep answers short. If someone reports a problem, offer a clear next step such as a call or a return visit instead of defending yourself in public. Facility managers and homeowners both read the replies when choosing. Ask for reviews at a natural moment, such as when you hand over a finished move-out, and send a direct link so it takes seconds. Never exchange discounts for reviews, because Google treats that as a violation.

Categories for houses and for buildings

Many Edmonton cleaning companies do both homes and commercial sites, and the profile should reflect the work you want most. Pick one primary category that matches it, then add the others as secondary categories and as separate services with short descriptions. A janitorial buyer and a family looking for a weekly cleaner should each see something that fits. Link the profile to the page on your website that best matches the main service. Should I list St. Albert and Sherwood Park on my Edmonton profile?

Yes, if you genuinely work there. Add them as service areas rather than creating extra profiles. If you do not serve them, leave them off, because inaccurate areas can confuse customers and attract a review by Google.

How do commercial buyers use my profile?

They look for services, reviews and signs that you are established. Add office and janitorial services, a clear description and a website link to a commercial page that covers insurance, supervision and scheduling.

When is the best time to refresh photos?

Before your busy seasons, often late winter ahead of spring cleaning. Use real photos of your crew, equipment and finished work, and get customer permission when a home or workplace is visible.

Can I use one profile for house and office cleaning?

Usually yes, if it is one business. Choose the main category for your core work, add secondary categories and list each service clearly. Make sure your website has matching pages for both kinds of customer.

What should I do when a review is unfair?

Reply politely and factually. If it breaks Google's rules, such as a fake review or abuse, you can report it, but Google decides. A calm reply often reassures future customers more than the removal of one review.

Can I hide my home address?

If customers do not visit you, yes. Google allows hiding the address and showing service areas instead. We set it up correctly.

What if my profile was suspended?

Suspensions usually come from rule violations such as a wrong address or a name with extra keywords. We review the cause, correct it and submit a reinstatement request.
