Why branding matters for a cleaning company

Cleaning is a trust business. Customers let you into their homes and workplaces, often while they are out. Before they call, they judge you in seconds from your logo, your website, your van and your Google profile. A brand that looks consistent and careful signals reliability. One that looks thrown together can make a good company look risky.

Branding is also practical. A logo that works on a van door, an embroidered uniform and a small phone icon makes you easier to remember and easier to recommend. A consistent look across your website, social pages and invoices helps referrals stick.

What a branding project includes

  • Logo concepts and a final logo with horizontal, stacked and icon-only versions.
  • A colour palette and font choices that work on screen and in print.
  • A short brand guide covering logo use, colours, tone of voice and examples.
  • Profile images and covers for your website, Google Business Profile and social pages.
  • Files prepared for printers, sign shops and uniform suppliers.

Our branding process

We begin with a discovery call to learn who you serve, how you want to be seen and who you compete with. We then present directions on real mockups, such as a van, a uniform and a website header, so you can judge them in context. You choose one direction, we refine it through a few rounds and then build the full kit. We finish by helping you apply the new brand to your website, profile and print items.

Designing for how cleaning brands are actually used

Cleaning logos appear in tight spaces: a polo chest, a door decal, a social avatar. Fine detail disappears at that size, so we design simple marks and test them small. We choose colours that feel clean and trustworthy, avoid cliches that make you look like every other cleaner and keep enough contrast for readability.

Branding and your website

A new brand should flow into your site, not sit beside it. We plan branding and website design together where possible, so colours, fonts and imagery match. If you already have a site, we can update it to fit. A consistent profile on Google also helps, and we can refresh it as part of our Google Business Profile work.

Refresh or full rebrand?

If customers already recognise your logo, a refresh that keeps its best parts may be smarter than starting from scratch. If your name, market or offer has changed, a full rebrand may make sense. We recommend what fits your situation and quote after a discovery call.

What we need from you

  • A description of your main customers: homes, offices or both.

How much does cleaning company branding cost?

It depends on whether you need a logo refresh or a full identity with guidelines. We confirm a clear quote after a discovery call, with no surprise add-ons. Our fixed website packages are on the pricing page.

Can you redesign my existing logo instead of starting over?

Yes. If your current logo has equity with customers, we can modernise it and keep what people already recognise.

Will the logo work on vans, uniforms and signs?

Yes. We supply versions for dark and light backgrounds, single-colour print and embroidery, and files your sign shop can use.

Do you work with cleaning companies outside Calgary?

Yes. Calgary is our main market, and we work remotely with cleaning companies across Canada by video call and email.

How long does a branding project take?

Most cleaning company brand projects take two to four weeks, depending on how quickly feedback comes back.

How long does a branding project take?

A logo refresh is quicker than a full identity with guidelines. We agree a timeline after the discovery call, and the main variable is how quickly feedback arrives.

Will I own the logo?

Yes. Once the project is paid for, you receive the final files and the rights to use them for your business.

Can you design vehicle wraps and uniforms?

We supply the files and guidance your printer or sign shop needs. We do not produce the physical items, but we help you brief the supplier.

Do I need a brand guide?

A short guide is helpful once more than one person produces materials, because it keeps the look consistent. We keep it simple and easy to use.

Can you update my website to match the new brand?

Yes. We can apply the new look to your website and profiles as a follow-on project, or alongside the branding work.
