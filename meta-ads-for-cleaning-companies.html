How Facebook and Instagram ads differ from search

Google ads catch people who are already looking for a cleaner. Facebook and Instagram ads work the other way round. You show your offer to people who match a profile, such as homeowners in your service area, before they ever type a search. The customer was not planning to hire anyone, and your ad puts the idea in front of them.

That makes the creative and the offer matter much more. An ad has to earn attention in a busy feed, so a clear photo of real work, a simple message and an easy next step count for a lot. It can build awareness and bring enquiries, but it needs a different approach from search.

Which cleaning companies benefit most

Meta ads suit businesses with a visual story and a clear offer: a spring deep clean, a move-out special, a first visit trial or a carpet cleaning day. Residential companies and anyone serving homeowners tend to find it easier to use than strictly commercial ones. We would rather say so early than run a campaign that suits the platform better than your business. Some companies do better putting the same budget into search or their profile.

They are also useful for building local recognition, so that when someone does need a cleaner, your name already feels familiar. If your business relies on a single high ticket contract, or you have no way of following up quickly, the channel may not be the best place to start. We help you judge that honestly, usually by comparing it with Google Ads.

Our process from offer to enquiry

  • Pick one clear offer worth promoting, with a start and end date.
  • Define the audience by area, age range and interests that suit your service.
  • Create ad images and short videos from your real work and team.
  • Write copy that is plain, local and free of hype.
  • Connect the ad to a short lead form or a dedicated landing page.
  • Set up retargeting for people who visited the site but did not enquire.
  • Review results and swap out tired ads regularly.

What we need from you

Meta ads live or die on the material. We need photos and short videos of your crew at work, finished rooms, before and after shots and anything with a personality. Phone footage is fine if it is clear and well lit. Always get permission before showing a customer's home or a staff member's face.

We also need to know what you can offer and when. A promotion you cannot deliver damages your reputation. Tell us your capacity by week, the areas you want, and who will respond to leads. Fast replies matter even more here, because social leads are often made on impulse and cool quickly. A booking tool that confirms instantly can help.

Where Meta campaigns go wrong

  • Boosting a post instead of building a proper campaign.
  • Targeting too wide an area, so you pay for people you cannot serve.
  • Using stock photos that look like every other cleaner.
  • Asking for a long form when a short one would do.
  • Replying to leads the next day.
  • Running the same ad until nobody notices it any more.

Judging results sensibly

Impressions and likes are easy to count and rarely pay anything. We look at enquiries, the cost of each one and, most importantly, what happened afterwards. Lead form submissions on social tend to include more tyre kickers than a phone call from search, so quality needs checking alongside volume. A lower quantity of good leads usually beats a pile of poor ones.

We ask you to tell us which leads became jobs and feed that back into the targeting. We also keep expectations realistic: the numbers fluctuate, some offers land and some do not, and a month or two of testing is normal before the picture becomes clear. That patience keeps you from judging an offer on a few quiet days.

How Meta ads work with the rest of your marketing

Social ads and search ads reach customers at different moments, so they work well together. Someone sees your spring offer on Instagram, forgets about it, then later searches your name or a service and finds your Google Ads or your profile. Each touch makes the next one easier. Each touch makes the next one easier, and none of them has to do the whole job alone.

Your reputation online supports all of this. A page full of good reviews on your Google Business Profile gives curious people confidence when they look you up after seeing an ad. We aim to keep the messaging consistent across all of it so you look like one reliable company. That consistency is a quiet advantage that costs nothing extra.

What the first few months look like

Expect a testing period. We launch with a few different images, headlines and audiences and let the data show which ones respond. Early results can be uneven, and we avoid big conclusions from a few days of numbers. Meta ads ask for patience in the first weeks, and your feedback on lead quality speeds the learning up. Share what your team notices on calls, and we will use it to adjust the audience and the offer.

As the account matures, we cut weak ads, put more behind what works and refresh creative before it gets stale. Retargeting audiences also grow over time, which usually makes later months more efficient than the first. We keep you informed throughout, without any promise of a particular number of leads. Our updates stay short and clear, so you can read them between jobs.

What you can check. We make no promises on lead cost. Meta ad campaigns are scoped after a discovery call, and we report cost per lead and lead quality in plain language so you can judge them yourself.

Are Facebook ads good for cleaning companies?

Yes, especially for residential and recurring cleans. They work best with a clear offer, local targeting and fast follow-up.

Meta ads or Google Ads, which is better?

Google Ads reaches people already searching. Meta ads reach people before they search. Many cleaners use both, starting with the one that fits their goals.

What budget do I need for Meta ads?

Small tests can start modestly. We recommend a budget that produces enough data to learn, and we confirm it after a discovery call.

Do you handle the leads that come in?

We set up instant notifications and can supply a follow-up script so you can reply quickly. Fast replies improve results.

Do I need a website for Meta ads?

It helps a lot. A clear landing page turns ad clicks into quote requests, and a lead form can work while your site is built.

Do Facebook ads work for cleaning companies?

They can, especially for seasonal offers, recurring plans and move-out cleaning. They reach people before they search, so the creative and the offer need to be clear. We test and adjust based on the leads you actually receive.

How much should I spend on Meta ads?

Start with a budget that lets us test for a few weeks, then adjust. The right amount depends on your area, offer and capacity. We help you pick a sensible starting point.

Should I use a lead form or send people to my website?

Lead forms are quick and easy on a phone. A landing page can give more information and qualify leads better. We often test both.

Do you use my customer list for targeting?

Only with proper consent and in line with privacy rules. We can use your own data for retargeting or similar audiences where it is allowed and appropriate.

What if the leads are low quality?

We look at the offer, the creative, the form questions and the targeting, and we tighten them. We also look at how quickly leads are contacted, which affects how many turn into jobs.
