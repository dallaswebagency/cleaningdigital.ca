Schema Markup for Cleaning Companies: What It Is and What to Add

By Nakul Chandra, Founder and Lead Strategist · November 11, 2026 · 5 min read

Schema markup is a layer of structured data in your page code that tells search engines what your page is about. For a cleaning company, it helps Google and AI tools understand who you are, what you offer and what questions you answer. It is not magic, but it is part of a solid technical setup, and this site uses it on every page that needs it. It also supports our AI SEO work.

Business and organization details

Mark up your business name, website, logo, contact details and service area on your home page. This gives search engines a clear, consistent description of who you are, and it should match your Google Business Profile.

FAQ markup on pages with real questions

If a page includes a genuine FAQ section, FAQ markup describes those questions and answers in a format machines can read. Only mark up questions that are actually visible on the page. Every article on this blog follows that rule.

Breadcrumbs for site structure

Breadcrumb markup shows how a page sits in your site, such as Home, Blog, then the article. It helps search engines understand your structure and can improve how the result looks.

Article markup for blog posts

For posts, mark up the headline, author, dates and publisher. It tells search engines who wrote the content and when, which supports trust, especially when the author is a real person with a real profile.

What not to add

Do not mark up reviews you cannot show, services you do not offer or content hidden from visitors. Misleading markup can cause Google to ignore it, so keep it accurate and keep it matched to what people can see.

What schema is and why it is optional but useful

Schema markup is code that labels the information on a page so machines understand it: this is a business, here is its phone number, here are the questions on the page. It does not guarantee rankings or rich results, but it helps search engines and AI tools read your site accurately. Think of it as tidy labelling, not a trick.

Start with the basics

Add Organization or LocalBusiness markup with your name, website, phone number and service area. Keep it identical to what visitors see and to your Google Business Profile. Inconsistent details cause confusion more than missing schema does.

Match markup to visible content

Markup must describe content that is actually on the page. Do not mark up reviews you do not show or questions that are not on the page. Google can ignore or penalise misleading markup. Add FAQ markup only where the questions and answers are written out for visitors.

Test before and after

Use Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator to check your pages. Fix errors, then monitor the Enhancements section in Search Console. Remember that eligibility for rich results changes over time, so do not build strategy around any one feature. See our Search Console guide.

Keep it maintainable

Schema that is edited by hand on every page becomes outdated. Use a template or plugin that fills in details from one source. When your phone number or areas change, update them once. Our website design work builds structured data into the templates.

Which types to use for a cleaning business

LocalBusiness or a more specific type suits your main entity, with Service entries for what you offer, FAQPage where questions appear on the page, BreadcrumbList for navigation and Article for blog posts. Use the most specific accurate type and leave out fields you cannot fill truthfully.

Schema and AI tools

AI search tools read pages as well as markup. Clear writing, consistent details and structured data together make it easier for them to understand who you are and where you work. Do not expect markup alone to get you mentioned. Our AI SEO service looks at the whole picture.

When to ask for help

If your site is built on a platform that handles schema automatically, check the output. If it is custom, have someone review it after each major change. A mistake in the template can spread across every page.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does schema markup improve rankings directly?

Not directly. It helps search engines understand your content, which can support visibility and richer results, but it does not replace good content.

Do I need a plugin for schema?

On WordPress, plugins can help. On a custom or static site, schema is added directly into the page code. Either way it needs to be accurate.

How do I test my schema?

Google offers a Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator. Paste a URL or code and check for errors.

Is FAQ markup still worth adding?

Yes for clarity and AI search, even though Google now shows FAQ rich results for a limited set of sites. Visible FAQ content helps visitors regardless.

Does schema improve my rankings directly?

Google has said structured data is not a direct ranking factor, but it helps pages be understood and can make them eligible for some rich results.

Can I add schema myself?

Yes. Many plugins and templates do it, and Google provides testing tools. Check the output carefully.

Should I use JSON-LD?

Yes. JSON-LD is the format Google recommends because it keeps markup separate from the visible page.
