Reviews from students, landlords and families

Hamilton's customers include students, landlords, long-time homeowners and facility managers, and reviews from each group help a profile look credible. After a move-out, ask the student or landlord for a quick review with a direct link, since memories fade quickly. Reply to each review and mention the area or the type of job. When a review is critical, answer calmly and offer a solution. Do not offer rewards for reviews or ask friends to write them, because that breaks Google's rules and can put the profile at risk.

Seasonal photos and a tidy service area

End-of-term periods in late April and August are busy, so refresh photos and post a short move-out update a few weeks before. Spring brings window and carpet work in older homes, and winter brings salt on entries. Keep your service area to the parts of Hamilton and nearby places you genuinely serve. Can I win student move-out work from my profile?

A clear move-out service, a short description of what is included and a quick way to book all help. Students decide fast, so reviews, photos and a visible phone number matter. Keep posts timed to end-of-term periods.

Should I add Burlington to my Hamilton profile?

Only if your crews genuinely work there. Add it as a service area rather than a separate profile. If you do not serve it, leave it out so customers and Google see accurate information.

Is local SEO worth it in a mid-sized city like Hamilton?

Often yes. Competition is lighter than in Toronto, so a complete profile and clear pages can stand out. Results vary and take time, and no one can guarantee a ranking.

When do student move-outs peak in Hamilton?

Often at the end of the academic year and before new leases start. Check your own booking history to confirm, then refresh photos and posts a few weeks earlier so students can find you.

Is it fine to list areas I only occasionally visit?

It is better to list areas you serve regularly. Occasional work can be added later once you can offer reliable service and your website has matching information.

Can I hide my home address?

If customers do not visit you, yes. Google allows hiding the address and showing service areas instead. We set it up correctly.

What if my profile was suspended?

Suspensions usually come from rule violations such as a wrong address or a name with extra keywords. We review the cause, correct it and submit a reinstatement request.
